A premise relayed into a plan (a provider's limit, a price, a capability) shapes the whole plan the same way an unmeasured constraint in a comment does, and is as cheap to read first-hand. A plan whose goal rests on such a premise reads the primary source before the first step, and says so.

## Evidence

One carrier. An optimisation plan was framed around a hosting provider's free-plan request limit: cut requests per page to stay within it. Reading the provider's billing page first-hand showed that static assets were not counted; the work went ahead, but its goal changed from cost to speed, and the plan's rationale was rewritten. The candidate's first occurrence was a constraint written in a comment; this one is a premise relayed in a request.
