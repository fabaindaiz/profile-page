A failure planted once shows that a check can turn red, not that it covers every form its rule promises to cover: plant one failure per documented form. A check seen to fail on one input can still pass every other form the rule names, and the rule's own text is the list of forms to plant.

## Evidence

Two owner repositories, one owner. First repository: an audit that had been seen to fail once let through thirteen of thirteen failures planted by a later review, each of a different form that the rule said it covered; after the fixes all thirteen failed the check. Second repository: expected values in a test that were counted by hand instead of run were the cause in about six of ten sessions, with the rule already written down and not followed. Source: the session logs of both carriers.
